About

I am an architect (Bachelor of Architecture, Khulna University) and a 3D product designer. Through Designtech Architects & C.G.I. I take buildings and products from first idea to final presentation.

On architecture I handle concept, design, planning, CAD drafting, 3D modelling, rendering and working drawings. On products I model in SolidWorks and render and animate in KeyShot.
